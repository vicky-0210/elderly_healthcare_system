5. Core Scientific Novelties & Methodology Defense Matrix
Key Innovations Validated in Research Results
ECG: Absolute Voltage Preservation
Standard deep learning normalizes every ECG record with whole-file z-scoring, destroying absolute millivolt R-wave amplitudes—the primary biomarker for LVH. Our pipeline uses a rational polyphase FIR filter to preserve absolute physical millivolts while converting 500 Hz to 360 Hz.
IMU: Gravity Invariance & Weak Distillation
By estimating the 3D gravity vector and projecting dynamic acceleration and angular velocity into vertical and horizontal components, the classifier is mathematically invariant to mounting rotation. A weak witness teacher stabilizes trial-level learning, preventing seed collapse.
EDA: MC-SAED Asymmetric Decomposition
Replaces non-causal convex optimization (cvxEDA, taking seconds) with a causal asymmetric exponential filter. Rapid sympathetic sweat surges are captured without pulling up tonic baselines, achieving 0.8986 macro F1 with personalized resting calibration.
PPG: Mean-Preserving Haar Shrinkage
Optical PPG contains high-frequency cardiac pulses and slow respiratory modulations. Pair-mean-preserving Haar wavelet denoising eliminates sensor noise without attenuating low-frequency respiratory baseline wander, increasing patient AP by +0.041.
