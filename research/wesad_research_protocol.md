# WESAD wrist stress-associated arousal study

The [completed study report](results/wesad_loso_v1/README.md) presents the 145 trained configurations, model choices, held-out checks, artifacts and deployment limitations. The final three longer-budget neural runs and the timing-only control are exploratory analyses, separate from the original candidate selection.

## Question and population

Can an unseen participant's wrist skin conductance distinguish the WESAD resting baseline from the Trier Social Stress Test? Does motion-conditioned signal processing add information beyond ordinary decomposition, and how much of the classification can the other wrist channels explain? The unit of generalization is a participant. A 60-second prediction describes a protocol-associated physiological state. Exercise, illness, medication, ambient conditions and electrode contact can also affect physiology. The original study reported an age of 27.5 ± 2.4 years across 15 participants. Evaluation on older adults wearing the intended device is a separate study. [Schmidt et al., dataset and original evaluation](https://kristofvl.github.io/usi/pdf/ubi_icmi2018.pdf)

## Prior work and consequences for this experiment

| Primary work | What the authors tested | Implication here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Schmidt et al., WESAD](https://kristofvl.github.io/usi/pdf/ubi_icmi2018.pdf) | Wrist and chest features, classical classifiers and participant-level validation. Their binary task grouped baseline and amusement as the negative class. They also reported wrist motion as a distinct comparator. | Use the synchronized wrist release. Keep baseline vs stress separate from broader negative-class definitions. Re-evaluate modality effects on identical people and windows. |
| [Choi et al., ambulatory stress monitor](https://psi.engr.tamu.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/choi2012titb.pdf) | Estimated a smooth trend by penalizing its second differences and obtained phasic activity as the signed residual (Eq. 1). This is the decomposition cited in the WESAD paper. | Compare against the cited estimator and document the choice of smoothing penalty at 4 Hz. |
| [Greco et al., cvxEDA implementation and paper](https://github.com/lciti/cvxEDA) | Expressed conductance as a smooth tonic component, a phasic response to a sparse nonnegative driver, and a residual. | Use the original solver with a 0.25 s sample interval as an offline reference. Report solver handling, boundaries and runtime. |
| [Zhu et al., wrist EDA across four datasets](https://pspachos.net/pubs/JBHI2023.pdf) | Evaluated component-derived features and classical learners on wrist recordings. Their 30-second non-overlapping segments and merged negative labels differ from the primary task here. | Include strong classical baselines, quantify how window choice affects results, preserve the measured 4 Hz signal and avoid fine-grained timing claims. |
| [SELF-CARE, context-aware wrist sensor fusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08215) | Used sensor context, including wrist motion, to guide fusion on WESAD. | Motion-conditioned fusion has prior art. Test the added value of conditioning EDA reliability separately from letting a classifier recognize movement. |
| [MultiWave, multiscale wearable-signal learning](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v209/deznabi23a/deznabi23a.pdf) | Learned representations across frequency components and wearable channels, including WESAD. | Compare multiscale models with single-scale and inexpensive feature baselines. |
| [Individualized stress mobile sensing with self-supervised pre-training](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11540419/) and [masked signal modeling](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1746809426001618) | Used self-supervised representation learning for wearable affect and stress tasks. | Compare pretraining with its supervised counterpart using only development participants. Masking or attention alone cannot establish originality. |
| [Aydoğan and Villagra Povina, exercise confounding](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42710527/) | Tested specificity under physical exertion as well as performance when separating laboratory rest and stress. | Measure context-only controls, report sensitivity and false alarms separately and require an activity challenge on the deployment population. |

**Candidate contribution.** MC-SAED combines a continuous causal estimate of tonic/phasic activity with joint evidence of motion and EDA corruption, then retains the reliability estimate as an observable input. PG-MSTN asks whether reliability-gated tonic, phasic, motion and feature branches improve held-out performance. The contribution being tested is the incremental value of these choices together and separately on this wrist protocol. The literature search establishes related work, while the ablations test whether the additions earn their complexity.

## Design fixed before comparison

- Use the 15 released participant IDs. Each subject supplies one complete held-out recording. Define groups by identity before segment selection, and build each subject's windows separately. Absolute timestamps, subject IDs and annotation codes stay outside model features. Each scored 60 s window advances by 15 s. At least 80% of original annotation samples must support the target condition. Separate three- and four-condition runs add amusement and meditation respectively.
- Hold out an explicitly resting calibration interval and exclude its windows in both protocols. Zero calibration uses no subject-specific reference from the held-out participant. Rest calibration uses the reserved resting interval. Compute learned preprocessing statistics, features requiring a fit, class weights, SSL weights and tuning choices exclusively within the training partition. Report causal rolling normalization as a separate sensitivity analysis.
- Each outer fold uses the next participant and the eighth subsequent participant in cyclic sorted ID order as a grouped inner validation pair. Use the remaining 12 for development, then refit on the 14 outer-training participants. Average macro F1 across the two inner participants for model tuning. Classifier decision is calibrated argmax. Inner calibration data may serve more than one tuning decision, making the outer evaluation essential.
- For each calibration protocol, compare all fixed configurations descriptively. A separate nested estimate picks the eligible primary binary configuration with highest **inner** macro F1 independently in each outer fold. The deployment rule tolerates up to 0.01 less inner macro F1, then prefers fewer required sensors, smaller model-file bytes, and the experiment ID to resolve ties. Report both rules. For the all-participant development export apply the same deployment rule to the mean inner score and median fold size across configurations in the zero-calibration protocol. Export scores on a training recording are replay checks only. Restrict final application to causal preprocessing on completed windows. No outer labels enter the selection rule.
- Report the mean and sample SD of participant metrics with percentile participant-bootstrap intervals. Binary AUROC, average precision, Brier and precision/recall/F1 target stress. In multiclass tables the same columns are macro one-vs-rest except Brier, which is the mean summed squared probability error. Calculate paired differences on common participants for matched design contrasts, bootstrap participants and correct paired Wilcoxon p-values with Holm's procedure. Differences between short, overlapping windows within one participant cannot be treated as independent samples. LOSO training sets overlap, so the intervals describe variability within this cohort.
- Benchmark CPU latency separately from the 60 s observation window, acquisition latency, feature extraction and Jetson latency. Report RAM scope and input modalities. Missing required sensors should produce an unavailable score. Hardware-domain validation must cover raw units, placement, synchronization, quality, exercise-associated false positives and representative older wearers before caregiver-facing alerts.
