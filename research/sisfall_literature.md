# SisFall fall-detection evidence review

This targeted review covers the release paper, studies using SisFall, and adjacent methods relevant to weak labels and state-space encoders. Published results below retain the authors’ task, population and split. **UV** means a detail could not be verified from the primary text examined. A source-cohort count of 38 is distinct from an experiment's analyzed sample count. Accuracy, precision, recall and F1 are percentages unless stated otherwise.

## Architectural and supervision precedents

InceptionTime established long parallel kernels with residual connections and an ensemble of independent networks. Our benchmark uses one slim six-module member with kernels 10, 20 and 40 at a 20 Hz evidence rate. Its parameter count and training budget belong to this experiment. [InceptionTime paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.04939)

Mamba's input-dependent state update motivates the CNN–selective-state-space comparison. Here a depthwise local kernel, input-dependent step, B/C projections and diagonal recurrence use a transparent PyTorch scan. The optimized upstream Mamba kernels were unavailable on the workstation. In adjacent wearable work, a dual-stream Kalman/Transformer study on SmartFallMM reports subject-held-out F1 91.10 and compares with CNN–Mamba. SmartFallMM uses a 32 Hz smartwatch. No verified six-channel SisFall result for that specific Mamba model is imported. [Mamba](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00752) · [Wearable comparison](https://www.mdpi.com/2504-2289/10/3/90)

Multiple instance learning supplies a bag label when the informative instance is unknown. Attention weights and witness scores require independent temporal evidence before interpreting them as impacts. Our inner attention summarizes a completed window, while the bag maximum supervises the trial. These operate at different scales. [Attention-based MIL](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/ilse18a.html)

## The research question this study can answer

* **Filtering and peaks.** The original release paper documents improvements and losses across features. Preserve native-rate extremum evidence alongside the adaptive filtered path and measure the ratio of filtered to native peaks on precisely the same recordings.
* **Orientation and fusion.** Dual streams, gating, attention, wavelets and TCNs each have prior art. Test the combination of a shared, co-rotation-invariant gravity frame, an unfiltered impact bypass, sequential multiresolution evidence and weak trial supervision. A common rigid coordinate rotation has an analytical invariance test. New attachment sites change the physical motion, requiring recordings.
* **Evaluation leakage.** Subject-separated studies exist. For studies whose split unit is UV, leakage is a possibility and cannot be inferred as a finding. Here trial duration alone classifies the local release perfectly. Grouping participants alone cannot remove an acquisition shortcut shared by everyone. Compare fixed exposure, whole-trial maxima and chronological ADL alarms explicitly.
* **Embedded use.** Validate model conversion, sensor gain and bandwidth sensitivity, false alerts per monitored hour and repeat-seed stability. Published model-forward times on other implementations cannot stand in for a measurement on this device.

The proposed contribution is a reproducible combination, controls and deployment evaluation. The current search does not establish priority or superiority across datasets. The empirical comparisons and failed variants are in the [research report](results/sisfall_v1/REPORT.md).
