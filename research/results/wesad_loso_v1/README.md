# WESAD wrist arousal study — results

**Use wrist EDA and accelerometry for the first hardware research prototype.** Without a resting calibration, the validation-based deployment rule selected a 31-coefficient logistic model on MC-SAED features. Its mean participant macro F1 was **0.873**, with **82.9% stress sensitivity** and **92.4% baseline specificity**. If two minutes of explicit resting calibration are practical, the strongest deep model by grouped validation was a reliability-aware temporal fusion network on those same two sensors. It achieved **0.899 macro F1**.

**The laboratory schedule is a major confound.** A control trained on elapsed session time alone reached **0.999 macro F1** on held-out people. The wearable models receive physiological features and sensor measurements only. This control shows how strongly the protocol orders baseline and stress. Movement-only features also scored **0.850**. Use the outputs as estimates of stress-associated physiological arousal under the WESAD protocol. Their specificity in older adults going about daily activities remains open. [Timing control](clock_control.csv) · [Context challenge](protocol_challenge.csv)

## What ran

The study used 24.13 hours from all 15 released participants aged 24–35. The 700 Hz synchronized annotations yielded **1,008 baseline and 628 stress windows** after reserving the resting interval. Each window spans 60 seconds and advances by 15 seconds. The 145 trained configurations include 69 classical and 76 neural experiments, each with 15 held-out participants. The comparison also reports four nested selection estimates. The 15 original subject pickle hashes matched after extraction. [Dataset audit](preprocessing/audit_summary.json) · [Methods and prior work](../../wesad_research_protocol.md)

For each test participant, 12 other people provided the inner training data and two provided validation. The selected variant was refitted on all 14. Scalers, class weights, pretraining and probability calibration used development participants. Rest-calibrated inference additionally received the designated two-minute resting interval for its subject. Both protocols scored the same primary windows. Each person contributes equally to the numbers below. [All 145 model configurations](model_comparison.md) · [Metrics, SDs and intervals](all_models.csv) · [Individual participants](per_subject.csv)

## What the proposed preprocessing and network contributed

MC-SAED processes each person's continuous 4 Hz conductance trace. A trailing derivative/MAD measure, contact plausibility and optional 32 Hz wrist movement estimate the reliability of each update. A causal asymmetric tracker updates tonic conductance, a smoothed positive excursion estimates phasic activity, and the feature extractor keeps absolute conductance, component changes, SCR morphology and quality. A resting capture supplies relative views only in the calibrated protocol. PG-MSTN combines a dilated tonic TCN, multiscale phasic TCN, motion/quality encoder and handcrafted-feature branch through a reliability-conditioned gate. [Signal processing and parameters](../../../modules/eda/docs/preprocessing.md) · [Neural architectures](../../../modules/eda/docs/neural.md)

- **Decomposition and gating.** Adding the tonic/phasic channels to PG-MSTN with its feature branch disabled increased mean macro F1 by .123 (unadjusted 95% interval .025–.225). The quality-conditioned gate gained .061 over ordinary learned gating. Adding the feature branch gained .059 and multiscale phasic convolution gained .051. None of the 13 study contrasts met the Holm-adjusted .05 threshold. These components are candidates for replication. [All A1–A10 contrasts, signed effects and adjusted tests](paired_contrasts.csv) · [Ablation figure](component_ablations.png)
- **MC-SAED versus established decompositions.** With EDA-only random forest, macro F1 was .825 for MC-SAED, .820 for cvxEDA, .818 for the sparse innovation method, .810 for Choi and .801 for the no-decomposition control. The MC-SAED–cvxEDA difference was .005 (−.034 to .036). With the same EDA + motion PG backbone, retrospective cvxEDA reached .852 versus .841 for MC-SAED. MC-SAED's clearest practical advantage is its causal operation. Measured decomposition cost averaged about .26 ms per minute of signal versus 8.29 ms for blockwise cvxEDA on this host. All 731 audited cvxEDA optimizations converged. [Method diagnostics](preprocessing/decomposition_summary.csv) · [Signal examples](preprocessing/protocol_examples.png)
- **Noise and physiological fidelity.** With .03 µS prominence, MC-SAED retained a phasic peak within one second of 75.2% of corresponding raw peaks, compared with 66.8% for cvxEDA and 89.7% for Choi. Raw peaks provide a proxy and may themselves contain artifacts. Adding motion to the quality estimator changed random-forest macro F1 by −.002 on the same inputs, and only .022% of samples had low motion-conditioned quality on average. Verified artifact labels and direct SCR ground truth would be needed to establish physiological fidelity. [Per-participant diagnostics](preprocessing/decomposition_by_subject.csv)
- **Pretraining and constrained learning.** Masked pretraining changed zero-calibration multi-scale TCN macro F1 by −.012 and contrastive pretraining changed tonic–phasic attention by +.009. The physics loss changed classification macro F1 by −.001 on the same learned refiner. It reduced mean total reconstruction RMSE from .375 to .227 µS relative to the unregularized refiner, while the continuous MC-SAED input itself had .017 µS component reconstruction RMSE. Better reconstruction of the learned representation did not yield a classification gain. [Learning curves](verification/learning_curves.png) · [Held-out learned components](verification/learned_components.csv)
- **Optimization.** In the initial six-epoch comparison, eight of 15 EDA + ACC PG inner fits chose epoch six. Extending the limit to 18 while preserving subjects, seeds and patience changed PG macro F1 from .841 to .863 for EDA + ACC and from .820 to .837 for full wrist. EDA-only multi-scale TCN changed from .737 to .721. These exploratory runs are reported separately from the frozen selection menu. The EDA + ACC gain was .022 (−.006 to .062). [Budget comparisons](exploratory_contrasts.csv)
- **Windows and normalization.** In the EDA-only random forest, 30/60/90 s windows scored .808/.825/.835 macro F1. The 30 s hop scored .815, 100% purity scored .834 and a causal rolling reference scored .824. Changing the window geometry also changes the eligible examples. For the default geometry, no additional low-pass, 0.65 Hz and 1 Hz scored .805/.825/.818. Global scaling used training participants. The only retrospective per-person reference allowed in the formal calibrated protocol was the explicitly reserved resting interval. [Complete preprocessing comparison](model_comparison.md)

## Specificity and secondary tasks

The timing control fitted a single threshold on window midpoint using only the 14 training participants. Its strong result reflects an opportunity to exploit task order. The ACC-only random forest reached .850 macro F1, and temperature alone reached .735. In the nested zero-calibration lightweight experiment, stress sensitivity was .780 in the lowest movement third and .886 in the highest. Motion thresholds were derived from the other participants. Sample composition changed across those strata. [Motion strata and class support](motion_strata.csv)

Applying each held-out binary model to the other two protocol states produced stress calls on **26.9% of amusement windows** and **14.7% of meditation windows**, averaged per person. These are out-of-task call rates. Separately trained PG-MSTN models reached .612 macro F1 for the three-condition problem (1,970 windows) and .575 for four conditions (2,684 windows). Adding affective states is considerably harder than the primary binary task. [Specificity challenge by participant](protocol_challenge_subjects.csv) · [Challenge plot](protocol_challenge.png)
